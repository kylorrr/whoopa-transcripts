No, the firmware is not the cheat itself.

In a DMA setup, the firmware is a specialized program flashed onto the physical DMA card itself. Its purpose is to ensure the card emulates a legitimate device (like a standard network or graphics card) so that system anti-cheats (like EAC) cannot detect that a DMA card is reading your system's memory.

The cheat or radar software itself runs separately on your secondary (radar) PC, communicating through the DMA card.
